fooyin 0.15.1 – MCTape & MCcut

Bring the classic cassette experience to your digital music player! 📼🎵

This release combines fooyin 0.15.1 with MCTape, a custom cassette animation plugin, and MCcut, a companion tool.

MCTape brings the nostalgic look and feel of a traditional audio cassette deck to fooyin, combining animated cassette reels with modern digital music playback.
